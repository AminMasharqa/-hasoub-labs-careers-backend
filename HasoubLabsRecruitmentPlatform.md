# **HasoubLabs Recruitment Platform**

### **Requirements & Feature Specification**

## **1\. User Roles & Access Control**

* **Three distinct user types:**

  * **HasoubLabs Employees/Admins:** Manage platform, oversee candidates, and handle system administration.  
  * **Candidates:** Job seekers looking to improve their profiles, apply for jobs, and connect with peers.  
  * **Seniors / Job Posters:** Mentors and senior professionals who can post job openings and share referral links.  
* **Geographic Verification:** Strict enforcement and validation to ensure all platform users are Israeli residents.

## **2\. AI & Profile Optimization**

* **Resume (CV) Improvement Engine:** Automated tool to analyze resumes and suggest structural and content improvements.  
* **Job-Specific CV Tailoring:** A feature that analyzes a specific Job Description (JD) and provides targeted suggestions to adjust the candidate's CV for better matching.  
* **LinkedIn Profile Enhancement:** Actionable suggestions and tips to optimize a candidate's LinkedIn presence.  
* **Automated JD Extraction:** The system automatically parses and extracts key requirements from posted Job Descriptions.  
* **Keyword Matching & Scoring:** The platform cross-references candidate profiles/CVs with extracted JD requirements, assigning an automatic rank/score to each candidate per position.

## **3\. Candidate Evaluation & Management**

* **Comprehensive Candidate Profiles:** Ability for authorized roles to view full candidate information, history, and metrics.  
* **CV Version Control:** Securely save and track all historical versions of a candidate's uploaded resumes.  
* **Multi-Stage Review System:** Continuous collection of evaluations, impressions, and feedback (not limited to a single review, but a complete timeline of reviews).  
* **Job-Specific Candidate Notes:** Add customized notes and feedback for a candidate applied to a specific role.  
* **Recruiter Handoff Workflow:** Collect reviews and notes, and send the consolidated candidate profile to the recruiter. Includes an option to edit the evaluation before sending the final email to the recruiter.  
* **Automated Self-Service Application:** Candidates can apply for roles independently through the system without requiring manual assistance from admins (e.g., Najwa).

## **4\. Communication & Integrations**

* **In-App Chat:** Direct messaging feature bridging Job Posters (Seniors) and Candidates, with an option to reveal the job poster's email address.  
* **Email Synchronization:** Full sync with core admin emails (e.g., Najwa's email) to log correspondence and notifications.
